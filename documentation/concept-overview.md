# Project Orion — Concept Overview

## Vision

A modern, intelligent, cloud-based Business Operating System that enables small and medium-sized businesses to manage their entire operations from a single platform — unifying procurement, accounting, inventory, invoicing, payments, CRM, HR, reporting, and analytics into one connected ecosystem. Designed for SMBs that need enterprise-level capability without enterprise-level complexity.

## The Problem

SMBs typically run on fragmented, disconnected tools (separate apps for accounting, invoicing, inventory, payroll, spreadsheets for reporting, messaging apps for internal comms), leading to inefficiency, errors, duplicated work, poor cash-flow visibility, and delayed decisions. Existing alternatives don't fit well:

- **Traditional ERP** — expensive, complex to implement, built for large enterprises.
- **Accounting software** — financial-records-only, weak on operations.
- **Small business tools** — disconnected from each other, limited automation.

## Product Philosophy (5 principles)

1. **Simplicity** — easy onboarding, simple interfaces, minimal training, mobile-friendly, clear workflows. Usable without an IT department.
2. **Intelligence** — AI is core, not a bolt-on. The system acts as an active assistant (e.g., explaining expense changes, recommending suppliers, auto-generating purchase orders) rather than just storing data.
3. **Automation** — configurable automated workflows (e.g., low-stock → purchase recommendation → approval → supplier order → inventory update → accounting entry; invoice generation → notification → payment tracking → accounting update).
4. **Integration** — connects to banks, payment providers, mobile money, e-commerce platforms, POS systems, payroll services, government systems, and third-party apps.
5. **Scalability** — cloud-native, multi-company, multi-branch, multi-user, multi-industry, supports regional/global expansion.

## Core Modules

| Module | Purpose | AI Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| A. Business Administration | Foundation: company profile, user mgmt, employee access, roles/permissions, departments, org settings | — |
| B. Procurement Management | Supplier mgmt, purchase requests, approvals, POs, supplier evaluation, purchase history, analytics | Supplier recommendations, cost optimization, purchase forecasting |
| C. Inventory Management | Product catalog, warehouses, stock tracking/transfers, barcodes, valuation, reorder mgmt | Demand forecasting, inventory optimization, overstock detection |
| D. Accounting & Financial Mgmt | General ledger, AP/AR, expense tracking, financial statements, tax, bank reconciliation | Expense categorization, financial forecasting, fraud detection, financial insights |
| E. Sales & Customer Mgmt | Customer database, sales pipeline, quotes, orders, communication, reporting | Customer behavior analysis, sales prediction, retention recommendations |
| F. Invoicing & Payments | Invoice creation/recurring, payment tracking, receipts, reminders, digital payments | Integrates with banks, payment gateways, mobile money |
| G. HR & Payroll *(future)* | Employee records, attendance, leave, payroll processing, performance tracking | — |
| H. Business Intelligence Dashboard | Revenue, expenses, profitability, inventory performance, sales performance, customer trends, cash flow | — |

## AI Business Assistant

A key differentiator: natural-language interaction with business data instead of menu navigation. E.g. "Show me my unpaid invoices", "Prepare a monthly financial report", "Which products are losing money?", "Predict next month's inventory needs."

## Target Market

- **Small businesses** (1–50 employees): retail, restaurants, professional services, clinics, schools, churches, NGOs.
- **Growing businesses** (50–250 employees): need more automation, better reporting, multi-location, more users.

## Competitive Positioning

Differentiates via: AI-first design, modern UX, industry-specific workflows, affordable pricing, strong automation, an integrated ecosystem, and regional adaptability.

## Technology Vision

- **Frontend**: modern web app, mobile-responsive.
- **Backend**: modular services, API-first architecture.
- **Database**: secure relational database.
- **Cloud infrastructure**: containerized deployment, automated scaling, monitoring, backups.
- **Security**: encryption, role-based access, authentication controls, audit logs, data protection.

*(These are directional; concrete stack/architecture decisions are tracked separately as they're made.)*

## Long-Term Vision

Beyond ERP: a Business Intelligence and Automation Platform. Future directions include industry-specific solutions, an integrations marketplace, a developer ecosystem, an AI-powered financial advisor, automated business planning, predictive analytics, and embedded financial services.

## Success Measurement

- **Product KPIs**: uptime >99.9%, fast response times, user adoption, automation usage, customer satisfaction.
- **Business KPIs**: active businesses, MRR, retention, CAC, revenue growth.
- **Customer Impact KPIs**: reduced admin workload, improved financial visibility, fewer operational errors, faster decisions, increased profitability.
